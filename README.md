## 🎓 About Vyntrax Prep

Vyntrax Prep is a comprehensive JEE Main & Advanced exam preparation platform designed to help aspiring engineers master all three subjects: **Mathematics**, **Physics**, and **Chemistry**. Built by NTA students for NTA students, our platform provides:

- 📚 **Complete Study Materials** - Chapter-wise and concept-wise theory notes
- 🧪 **Full-Length Mock Tests** - Simulate actual JEE exam environment
- 🗺️ **Personalized Study Maps** - Clear backlogs with structured learning sequences
- 📊 **Detailed Analytics** - Track your progress and performance
- 🌙 **Dark Mode Support** - Easy on the eyes during late-night studies
- 👨‍💼 **Admin Dashboard** - Monitor user activities and platform statistics
